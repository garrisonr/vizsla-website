Grizz Line — Message Program Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026 · Leesburg, Florida

The Grizz Line is the personal work text line of Richard Garrison, Onboarding Coordinator for Morgan Auto Group, sending from +1 352-531-2029. It sends one-to-one text messages to individual job candidates about their own application and onboarding: welcome messages, reminders about required onboarding documents, orientation reminders, and first-day logistics. It is not a marketing program and sends no bulk messages.

How you opt in

You opt in by providing your mobile phone number on your job application with Morgan Auto Group and agreeing to be contacted about your application and onboarding, or by texting this number first. Only people who provided their number and agreed to be contacted receive messages.

Information collected

The program uses the information you provided during the hiring process: your name, your mobile phone number, and details about your application and onboarding status (position, store, start date, and which onboarding documents are still needed). Message content you send to this line is also kept as part of the conversation record.

How information is used

Your information is used only to communicate with you about your application and onboarding, and to keep a record of those communications. It is never used for marketing.

Sharing — the mobile data promise

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Your number is passed only to the messaging carriers and platform providers that physically deliver the messages, solely so the messages can be delivered.

Retention

Conversation records are kept while your onboarding is active and for a reasonable period afterward as a business record of the hiring process. You may ask for your messages to be deleted at any time by contacting the number or email below.

Messaging terms

Message frequency varies by onboarding stage; most candidates receive fewer than 10 messages in total. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time — you will receive no further messages. Reply HELP for help, or call +1 352-531-2029. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
