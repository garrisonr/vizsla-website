Privacy

Privacy, in plain language

This website collects nothing: no cookies, no trackers, no analytics. The one place Vizsla holds anyone’s information is the newsletter below — and there, the rule is simple: your address is used to send you the newsletter, and for nothing else, ever.

This website

vizslaos.com is a set of static pages. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party trackers. The pages are served by GitHub Pages, which — like any web host — keeps ordinary server logs; we do not add anything to them. The one form on the site is the newsletter signup, covered next.

The newsletter — Vizsla Post

The Vizsla OS newsletter is sent by Vizsla Post, our own newsletter tool, running on our own server. This section is its policy.

What we collect

  • Your email address — required, it’s where the newsletter goes.
  • Your name, only if you choose to give one on the signup form. It personalizes the hello; nothing more.
  • The dates and times of your signup and of your confirmation click — the double opt-in record that proves you asked to be on the list.
  • Unsubscribe and complaint events, if they happen — so we never mail you again.

That is the whole list. We do not buy, rent, append, or enrich subscriber data from anyone.

What it is used for

Only to send you the Vizsla OS newsletter: release news and letters from the workshop, when there is news worth sending. Your information is never sold, rented, or shared with anyone, for any purpose. It is passed only to the mail service that physically delivers the email (today, our Microsoft 365 mailbox), solely so the message can arrive.

Where it lives

On our own server in Leesburg, Florida, in the newsletter tool’s own database — not in a marketing cloud, and not on a platform that could hold it hostage.

Leaving, and being deleted

Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe that takes effect immediately — no sign-in, no survey, no hard feelings. After unsubscribing, your address is kept only on a suppression list whose single job is to make sure you are never mailed again. To have your information deleted entirely, write to admin@vizslaos.com and it will be done.

The postal address

Anti-spam law requires a real postal address in every issue’s footer. Ours is:
Vizsla OS · 615 N 14th St #106 · Leesburg, FL 34748

Questions

Anything about this policy, or about your information, goes to the same inbox: admin@vizslaos.com. A human — or the AI he directs — will answer.

Effective November 27, 2026. If this policy changes, the change will be posted here and noted in the newsletter before it takes effect.
