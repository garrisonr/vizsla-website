For Businesses

Compliance, built in.

Most HR software treats compliance as a marketplace: the safety sheets live in one subscription, the posters in another, the training in a third, and every one of them bills by the seat. IDenty Pro takes the opposite position. The documents are public, the law that requires them is public, and the software that keeps them current is ours — so the libraries ship inside IDenty Pro, at no charge, and the core courses are released under a Creative Commons licence for anyone to use.

The MSDS / SDS safety library

OSHA’s Hazard Communication rule is simple: a safety data sheet for every hazardous chemical in the building, readily accessible to every employee, every shift — no barriers. IDenty Pro’s SDS library is built to exactly that standard. It is an open shelf: search by the name on the label, browse by location, and the full sheet is on screen in seconds, with no login standing in the way of a person holding a leaking bottle.

The shelf arrives stocked. We pulled the manufacturers’ own published sheets for the products nearly every workplace buys — 45 of the most common commercial cleaning and maintenance products (Clorox, Lysol Professional, Dawn Professional, Windex, Zep, and the rest of the janitor’s cart) — each filed with its source and revision date. Your own chemicals join them as an inventory, and IDenty Pro chases the rest: a product with no sheet, or a sheet gone stale, becomes a named task for a named person until it is filed.

Print any single sheet, or a location’s whole set, from the library itself.

The mandatory poster library

Every employer owes its walls a set of posters — federal wage and safety notices, and a different set again in every state. The library carries the federal set and all fifty states, each poster a versioned record with its issuing agency and effective date. IDenty Pro works out your required set from where your people actually work, so a Leesburg shop sees Florida’s wall, not fifty states of homework.

One click prints the entire current set for a location, led by a manifest page listing every poster and its version — the stack on the wall documents itself. And because thirty-odd states reissue their wage posters every January, the library re-checks its sources every December and June and flags anything stale or retired before an inspector does.

Core compliance courses, free — and free to keep

The core library is about a dozen classes, authored from the public primary sources — OSHA, DHS, the EEOC, NIST, and the FTC publish the substance; we wrote the courses. Included with IDenty Pro at no charge, and released under a Creative Commons licence (CC BY-SA): use them, adapt them, share them.

  • Active Shooter: In the Workplace
  • Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)
  • Bloodborne Pathogens: Standard Precautions
  • Cybersecurity for Employees
  • Data Privacy: Safeguards and Security
  • Discrimination and Bias
  • Jobsite Housekeeping
  • Red Flags Rule: Identity Theft Prevention
  • Sexual Harassment for Employees
  • Slips, Trips and Falls
  • Workplace Bullying

The formats fit the material instead of padding it: short native lessons, video where video earns its runtime, a quiz drawn from a real question bank, and — where the obligation is behaviour, not knowledge — a plain-language agreement the employee reads and signs, kept on their record. People who already know the material can test out. Assignments follow a role, location, and hazard matrix, so desk staff are never force-fed shop-floor modules, and every completion lands as an auditable fact with the course version attached.

What it costs

Nothing. The posters are published free by the governments that require them; the safety sheets are published free by the manufacturers who write them; the courses are Creative Commons. What the subscription vendors sell was never the documents — it is the chasing. Chasing is a workflow, and IDenty Pro is the workflow.
